📋 Before Contacting Support — Checklist

*   Confirm your Node.js version is **18 or higher**.
*   Ensure you ran `npm install` successfully before starting the app.
*   Verify the app is running before visiting `/setup`.
*   Check that your domain is correctly pointed to your server.
*   Make sure SSL is active if using payment gateways or social login.
*   Watch the relevant setup video (cPanel or aaPanel) before reaching out.

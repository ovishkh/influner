The User Panel is where creators do their work — building AI influencers, generating content, managing their gallery, and tracking usage. It's designed to be intuitive and fast.

*   Credit balance overview
*   Recent content activity
*   Quick-start shortcuts

#### 🧬 Influencers

*   Create custom AI personas
*   Manage existing models
*   Deploy across content types

#### 🖼️ Gallery

*   View all generated content
*   Download & share assets
*   Organize by project

#### 🎬 Content

*   Generate AI reels & videos
*   Product video creation
*   Platform-optimized output

## Platform Features

AI Influencer is packed with everything a modern AI content SaaS needs — from content generation to monetization and beyond.

* * *

🎬 AI Reel Creation

Generate hyperreal motion reels, complete with sound and captions, from a single text prompt. The platform produces social-ready video content in seconds — no editing skills required.

🧬 AI Model Builder

Design fully custom AI personas — choose any appearance, ethnicity, or style. Train the model on a unique identity and deploy it across videos, reels, ads, and product shoots. Unlimited content, zero reshoots.

🛍️ AI Product Models

Upload a product photo and let the AI place it in any scene, any style, any world. Perfect for e-commerce brands looking to generate stunning product visuals without expensive photoshoots.

🎭 AI Influencer Studio

Create a virtual influencer persona that generates viral dance reels, trending content, and social-ready videos on demand. Built for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts with auto-optimization per platform.

📝 Script to Video

Convert written scripts directly into fully produced video content. Ideal for creators who prefer to write their content before generating the final visual output.

💳 Credit-Based Monetization

Fully integrated subscription and credit system. Admins can configure plans, pricing, and payment gateways. Users purchase credits to generate content — a scalable SaaS revenue model out of the box.

🌐 Multi-Language & Localization

Full web translation management built into the admin panel. Reach a global audience by translating the entire platform interface into any language.

📊 Usage Analytics

Users can track their credit consumption and generation history. Admins get a full dashboard overview of platform activity, user growth, and revenue.
